🧁 What is coding-pudding?

coding-pudding is a free, open-source text editor and Python IDE specifically built for low-configuration laptops and PCs — the kind of older machines that struggle with heavy software. If your computer is slow, has limited RAM, or an older processor, coding-pudding is the perfect companion for your coding needs.

You don’t need to be a programmer to use it. It’s simpler than regular IDEs but way more powerful than plain Notepad. Think of it as a “smart Notepad for Python” — designed to be easy on your system yet still give you real coding features.
